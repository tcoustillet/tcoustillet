After obtaining a Bachelor's degree in biology and mathematics in 2019 at Sorbonne University (France), I completed a Master's degree in systems biology (bioinformatics) in 2021 at Sorbonne University. I'm interested in mathematical modelling as well as data analysis and algorithm design.

```console
tcoustillet@github:~$ cat about.txt ↴
```

After that, I spent two years working in computational toxicology as a research engineer within the [Systox](https://systox.u-paris-sciences.fr/) research group at Paris Cité University. My work focused on the exposome: I developed computational tools to understand the link between pesticide exposure in children and the socio-economic burden following neurodevelopmental disorders, as well as the link between exposure to environmental stressors and both breast cancer and Alzheimer's disease.

I then joined the [NeAR](https://www.ibps.sorbonne-universite.fr/en/research/development-adaptations-and-aging/Neural-adaptation-and-repair) team at Sorbonne University, where I spent two years working as an engineer in computational neuroscience. Cooperating closely with experimental researchers, I spent much of my time building computational tools that anyone can use, with no programming knowledge required. These included, among other things, the analysis of respiratory time series following spinal cord injuries.
